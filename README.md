CarbonCure Technologies provides innovative carbon removal technology that injects recycled CO₂ into concrete during mixing, permanently storing it while improving concrete strength. Founded in 2012, the company partners with concrete producers worldwide to reduce the carbon footprint of construction. Their platform offers a digital API for concrete producers to integrate CO₂ injection data, track usage, and report sustainability metrics, supporting green building certifications and regulatory compliance.

**Areas:** Company · Concrete · Sustainability · Carbon Removal · Construction
